## Teaching & Mentoring

### Teaching Experience

Throughout my PhD, I served twice as the **Instructor of Record** for AREN 3040: *Circuits for Architectural Engineers* at the University of Colorado Boulder, in Spring 2025 and Spring 2026.

As the sole instructor for the course, I was responsible for course instruction and management, including coordinating two graders who assisted with homework assessment. The course covers:

1. **Fundamentals of Electrical Circuits** — basic concepts of electrical circuits and current flow
2. **DC Circuit Analysis** — Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's current and voltage laws, superposition, Thevenin's theorem, and Norton's theorem
3. **Transient Analysis** — analysis of time-dependent behavior in DC circuits
4. **AC Circuit Analysis** — steady-state analysis and phasors
5. **Power Analysis** — reactive power, power factor, and single- and three-phase systems
6. **Transformers** — including three-phase transformers
7. **Electric Machines** — induction motors, with an introduction to synchronous and DC motors
8. **Digital Logic** — an introduction to digital logic

A copy of the course syllabus is available upon request.
